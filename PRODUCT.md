- Panther01Agency is the only approved brand name.
- Final services, sectors, metrics, testimonials, pricing, contact details, and social links are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Premium, corporate, cinematic tone.
- Red and near-black visual world.
- Controlled red light is a focal device, not a page-wide neon treatment.
- Avoid gaming, cyberpunk, hacker, Matrix, crypto, heavy glassmorphism, and template-like aesthetics.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate automation clearly without inventing results.

- Make all provisional content easy to replace.
